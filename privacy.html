Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. What the App accesses. When the owner logs in with TikTok, the App receives an access token and basic account information (open ID, display name) needed to upload videos to that same account.

2. How it is used. The data is used only to upload the owner's videos and check their upload status. It is not used for any other purpose.

3. Storage. Tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is stored on external servers, sold or shared with third parties.

4. Other users. The App does not collect data from any other TikTok users or viewers.

5. Deletion. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions); the local token file is then deleted.
